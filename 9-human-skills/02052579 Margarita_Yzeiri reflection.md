# Personal reflection

While researching this topic, I realized that AI is becoming a very powerful tool in software engineering. Before this assignment, I thought AI might replace many software engineers completely. However, after reading different articles and discussions, I understood that human skills are still very important.

What surprised me the most is how much companies still depend on communication, teamwork, and critical thinking. AI can generate code quickly, but it cannot fully understand human needs, emotions, or business goals.

One concern I have is that some developers may become too dependent on AI tools and stop improving their own skills. This could create problems if engineers no longer understand the code they use.

At the same time, I also see many opportunities. AI can help developers work faster, reduce repetitive tasks, and improve productivity. Engineers can focus more on creativity and solving complex problems.

I believe software engineers must continue learning and adapting to new technologies. In the future, I want to improve both my technical skills and my communication and problem-solving abilities so I can work effectively with AI tools instead of depending completely on them.

Overall, this research changed my opinion. I no longer think AI will fully replace software engineers. Instead, I think developers who combine human skills with AI knowledge will remain valuable in the future.
